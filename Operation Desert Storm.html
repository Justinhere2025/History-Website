About

Operation Desert Storm was a military campaign conducted by a U.S.-led coalition to liberate Kuwait from Iraqi Occupation.

Key Dates

  • January 17, 1991 — The air campaign begins.
  • February 24, 1991 — The ground campaign begins.
  • February 28, 1991 — A ceasefire is announced.
