What Happened: Operation Desert Storm

About

Operation Desert Storm was a Military Campaign and Combat Phase of the Pursin Gulf War led by the US and its allies, its Primary purpose was to Libarete Kuwait from iraqi passision.

Timeline of Operation Desert Strom

  • Cause: Iraq's invasion and occupation of Kuwait.
  • Start of Air Campaign: January 17, 1991
  • Start of Ground Campaign: February 24, 1991
  • Liberation & Ceasefire: Kuwait City was retaken in days, and a ceasefire was declared on February 28, 1991.

How Did they Achieve Their Goals?

The coalition forces achieved their goals through a combination of air and ground campaigns. The air campaign, which began on January 17, 1991, targeted key military installations and infrastructure in Iraq, weakening their ability to fight. The ground campaign, which began on February 24, 1991, quickly overwhelmed Iraqi forces and led to the liberation of Kuwait.

Facts

  • Coalition forces Overran Iraqi Forces in only 100 hours.
  • The air campaign consisted of a 42 day bombardment, invoving over 100,000 sorties.
  • Iraqi forces stationed in Kuwait: 180,000 to 630,000
  • Total Iraqi casualties: Approximately 8,000 to 50,000
